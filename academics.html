Learning pathways

Education for the Whole Learner

Our programmes develop knowledge, competencies, creativity and the confidence to face future opportunities.

CBC

Competency-Based Curriculum

The Competency-Based Curriculum develops the whole learner by building knowledge, practical abilities and positive values. It promotes creativity, communication, collaboration, critical thinking, problem-solving and digital literacy, preparing learners to confidently face future opportunities.

8-4-4

National Curriculum

The 8-4-4 curriculum continues to support strong academic achievement and excellent KCSE performance. Through committed teaching, regular assessment, guidance and disciplined study, learners are equipped for successful transition to universities and other higher-learning opportunities, with a 50%(+) university transition rate for our candidates.

2026 TARGET

KCSE Mean Grade 7.2

The Imperiums, our 2026 candidate class, are determined to carry the school flag high by achieving a KCSE mean grade of 7.2 With hard work, focused revision, teamwork, mentorship and support from teachers and parents, they are preparing to reach this important target.

The Road to 7.2

Every class runs its own lane toward the finish. Here's the season that takes The Imperiums from the starting block to results day.

Academic excellence

Academic Departments

Dedicated departments work together to give learners a broad and practical educational experience.

Science Department

The Science Department inspires curiosity, innovation, and practical problem-solving through Biology, Chemistry, and Physics offered by the school. Laboratory work, experiments, observation, research, safe use of resources, and continuous assessment help learners connect scientific knowledge to real-life situations.

HOD

Mr. Cosmas Kiio

Mathematics Department

The Mathematics Department develops logical thinking, accuracy, and problem-solving skills through Mathematics and, where offered, Essential or Core Mathematics. Clear teaching, regular practice, practical problem solving, continuous assessment, and feedback help learners build confidence and apply numerical skills in everyday life.

HOD

Mr. Bonface Mwangangi

Languages Department

The Languages Department strengthens communication, literacy, creativity, and cultural appreciation through English, and Kiswahili. Through reading, writing, speaking activities, debates, interactive lessons, and regular assessment, learners become confident and effective communicators.

HOD

Mr. Peter Nashon

Humanities Department

The Humanities Department helps learners understand society, culture, leadership, and their role in the world through subjects such as Social Studies, History and Government, Geography, and Religious Education. Discussions, research, projects, fieldwork, and current-affairs learning develop informed, responsible, and critical-thinking learners.

HOD

Mr. Michael Musoi

Technical & Applied Department

The Technical and Applied Department empowers learners with practical, creative, and future-ready skills through ICT, Computer Studies, Agriculture,and Music. Our strong ICT programme promotes digital literacy, coding, innovation, and responsible use of technology, while hands-on learning, projects, and continuous assessment help learners apply their skills confidently to real-life challenges.

HOD

Mr. Joseph Mwaniki

Games Department

We nurture teamwork, discipline, and talent through engaging sporting activities that support learners’ physical and social development. We achieve this through regular training, inclusive participation, qualified coaching, teamwork, and fair competition.

HOD

Mr. Onesmus Musila

Examinations Department

We promote academic excellence through fair, well-coordinated assessments that help learners measure progress and achieve their best. We achieve this through timely examinations, clear marking criteria, accurate record-keeping, and constructive feedback.

HOD

Mr. Daniel Nzioki

Boarding Department

We provide a safe, comfortable, and supportive home environment that promotes learners’ wellbeing, discipline, and independence. We achieve this through proper supervision, clear routines, hygiene standards, nutritious meals, pastoral care, and open communication with parents.

HOD

Mr.Edward Mulili

Guidance & Councelling Department

We provide caring support and guidance that helps learners build confidence, make positive choices, and thrive both personally and academically. We achieve this through confidential counselling, mentorship, life-skills sessions, parent collaboration, and early identification of learner needs.

HOD

Madam Ruth Mwewa
