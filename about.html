Our journey

A JOURNEY OF FAITH, GROWTH AND EXCELLENCE

St. Thomas Kavingoni Secondary School is a public, mixed secondary school located in Kathonzweni Sub-County, Makueni County. Established in 1983 by the Catholic Church as a mixed Harambee secondary school, the institution began with a humble enrolment of just 32 students and was initially known as Dr. Ugo Mazzone Secondary School. Since its establishment, the school has continued to benefit from the sponsorship of the Catholic Church, whose values and commitment to holistic education remain central to its identity and development.

Over the years, the school has steadily expanded its facilities and learning opportunities to meet the needs of its growing student population. In 1999, it introduced full boarding for Form Three and Form Four learners. The boarding facilities were subsequently expanded, allowing the majority of learners, including those in lower classes, to benefit from a structured environment that promotes academic focus, discipline and personal development.

The school has also recorded encouraging academic progress, consistently maintaining a mean score above 6.00 since 2021. This achievement reflects the dedication of its teaching staff, commitment of learners and continued support from parents and other stakeholders. Enrolment grew significantly from 2022, reaching 751 students in 2024 when the school operated four classes. In recognition of this growth and expanding capacity, the institution was registered as a four-stream school in 2024. Currently, the school has an enrolment of approximately 605 students.

The year 2026 marked another important milestone with the admission of the school's first cohort of 141 Grade 10 learners under the Competency-Based Education (CBE) framework. The school offers all three Senior School pathways, demonstrating its commitment to nurturing diverse talents, abilities and career aspirations. In the same year, the institution embarked on an ambitious initiative to acquire its first-ever school bus. Driven by the collective commitment of the Board of Management, parents, alumni, staff and the wider community, this initiative aims to enhance educational exposure, facilitate academic trips and support participation in sports and other co-curricular activities.

From its humble beginnings of 32 students in 1983 to its present identity as a growing institution embracing modern educational reforms, St. Thomas Kavingoni Secondary School has demonstrated remarkable resilience, steady progress and ambition. Today, the school remains firmly committed to its founding ideals of faith, service and holistic education while pursuing greater heights in academic excellence, discipline, talent development and character formation. Its story is one of humble beginnings, collective sacrifice, sustained growth and an unwavering belief in the transformative power of education.

Guided by our motto, "Education For A Better Life," we continue to nurture learners who can positively impact their communities, Kenya and the world.

Our foundation

What Makes Us Different

We build a learning environment where knowledge is strengthened by character, creativity and practical skill.

Academic Excellence

We provide a strong academic foundation that helps every learner build confidence, curiosity, and the ability to think independently. Through engaging teaching, consistent support, and practical problem-solving, students develop the knowledge and skills needed to perform well in school and prepare for future educational and career opportunities.

Innovation & ICT

We prepare students for a rapidly changing world by integrating digital literacy, technology, and innovation into their learning experience. From using modern digital tools to exploring coding, research, and creative solutions, learners gain the confidence to use technology responsibly and apply it to real-life challenges.

Talent Development

We believe that every student has unique abilities that deserve to be discovered, developed, and celebrated. Through sports, music, drama, clubs, leadership activities, and other creative opportunities, learners are encouraged to explore their interests, strengthen their talents, build teamwork skills, and grow in self-confidence.

Character & Leadership

We nurture responsible, respectful, and disciplined young people who are ready to make a positive difference in their communities. By promoting integrity, empathy, accountability, and service, we help students develop strong character and the leadership qualities needed to guide others with confidence and purpose.
