Who we are

Welcome to St. Thomas Kavingoni Senior School

We provide quality education in a safe, supportive and inspiring environment where every learner is encouraged to discover their abilities and reach their full potential.

Our approach combines strong academics with leadership, technology, sport, creativity, spiritual growth and community responsibility because a well-rounded learner is a better-prepared learner.

Our journey

A Proud History of Growth

St. Thomas Kavingoni School was established in 1983 with the vision of providing quality, holistic and affordable education to learners from Kavingoni and the surrounding community. What began as a modest institution with a small enrolment has grown into a respected centre of learning known for academic excellence, discipline, integrity and character.

Over the years, the school has grown through the commitment of dedicated teachers, supportive parents, the Board of Management, alumni, sponsors and the wider community — strengthening infrastructure, academic programmes and the learning environment year on year.

Today our educational experience combines academic learning with leadership development, spiritual growth, sport, co-curricular activities, environmental conservation, innovation and digital literacy. Learners are encouraged to become confident, responsible and compassionate citizens prepared for the modern world.

Guided by our motto, "Education For A Better Life," we continue to nurture learners who can positively impact their communities, Kenya and the world.

Our foundation

What Makes Us Different

We build a learning environment where knowledge is strengthened by character, creativity and practical skill.

Academic Excellence

We provide a strong academic foundation that helps every learner build confidence, curiosity, and the ability to think independently. Through engaging teaching, consistent support, and practical problem-solving, students develop the knowledge and skills needed to perform well in school and prepare for future educational and career opportunities.

Innovation & ICT

We prepare students for a rapidly changing world by integrating digital literacy, technology, and innovation into their learning experience. From using modern digital tools to exploring coding, research, and creative solutions, learners gain the confidence to use technology responsibly and apply it to real-life challenges.

Talent Development

We believe that every student has unique abilities that deserve to be discovered, developed, and celebrated. Through sports, music, drama, clubs, leadership activities, and other creative opportunities, learners are encouraged to explore their interests, strengthen their talents, build teamwork skills, and grow in self-confidence.

Character & Leadership

We nurture responsible, respectful, and disciplined young people who are ready to make a positive difference in their communities. By promoting integrity, empathy, accountability, and service, we help students develop strong character and the leadership qualities needed to guide others with confidence and purpose.
